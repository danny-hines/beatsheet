- **Chained edits:** OpenAI claims 2.5 holds instructions better over several rounds, but repeated edits can still change details. For regions that must stay pixel-identical, OpenAI advises compositing the approved edit back onto the original — [fal](https://fal.ai/learn/devs/gpt-image-2-5-vs-gpt-image-2). Models update without new version numbers, so their behavior can drift — [Rangy](https://rangy.ai/blog/seedream-5-pro-vs-nano-banana-pro-vs-gpt-image-2/)
- **Qwen-Image-Edit (2509 line):** artifacts with 3 references under true CFG, fine on Lightning (anecdote) — [community post](https://longform.social/post/did:plc:pgryn3ephfd2xgft23qokfzt/3ml4zt2einkv2)
- **Nano Banana 2 Lite:** "not optimized for multi-turn sequential editing" — [Gemini docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation)
- **Midjourney:** character and omni reference tools did not carry over to V8.1 and V8.2 — [ISSOH](https://www.issoh.co.jp/md/tech/11424)

### Inferences
- For beatsheet, chaining a sheet into many boards should always restart from the original sheet as the reference, not from the last board. This guards against drift (Rangy, OpenAI).
- When a shot needs exact color continuity (a costume color, say), GPT Image 2.x edits are reported to keep source colors better than Nano Banana Pro.

### Gaps
- GPT image models' known warm or yellow cast: whether GPT Image 2 or 2.5 fixed it is claimed only in pre-release leaks and forum posts, not in a test I could cite.
- No quirk reports were found for FLUX 3 Image, Muse Image, MAI-Image-2.6, Grok 2.0, Qwen-Image 3.0, Ideogram 4.5 or Krea 3. They are new (Jul–Oct 2026) and have little independent coverage.
- No source characterizes "plastic skin" for FLUX.2 or FLUX 3 in 2026.
